# Privacy

AI Strategy and Blueprint for PE Funds is a Claude plugin made by NXT Partners AI. This page covers the plugin only.

## What NXT Partners AI receives

Nothing. NXT Partners AI runs no server for this tool, and the plugin sends no data to NXT Partners AI or to anyone else.

## Where your answers go

Your answers stay in your Claude conversation, which Claude processes under your account's terms, and in one working file created in that conversation. You can ask to see or delete that file at any time.

## Two things that happen only if you choose them

- **Personalizing:** your firm's name is used in a public web search, and you confirm each fact it finds before it counts.
- **Writing to NXT Partners AI:** the tool drafts a short summary and you send it yourself. Nothing is sent from the chat.

## What the tool asks for

It does not need names of people, portfolio companies, LPs, or deals: ranges and general descriptions are enough. The only name it may ask for is your own firm's, and only if you choose to personalize.
